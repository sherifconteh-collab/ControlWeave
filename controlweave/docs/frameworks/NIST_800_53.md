# 📋 NIST SP 800-53 Guide

Using ControlWeave with the NIST Special Publication 800-53 framework.

## Overview

NIST SP 800-53 provides a catalog of security and privacy controls for federal information systems and organizations. ControlWeave seeds the Rev 5.2.0 catalog in full: all 20 control families, **300 non-withdrawn base controls and all 714 non-withdrawn control enhancements** (1,014 items), generated from the official NIST OSCAL catalog by `scripts/import-oscal-80053.js`. Enhancements carry `is_enhancement` and a `parent_control_id` link to the control they refine, taken from the OSCAL `rel="required"` relationship rather than parsed out of the identifier.

Each control also records its NIST SP 800-53B baseline membership, imported from the published Low/Moderate/High profiles: **149 controls at Low, 287 at Moderate, 370 at High** — of which 18, 110 and 182 respectively are enhancements. At the High baseline roughly half the required controls are enhancements, which is why baseline membership rather than raw catalog size is what compliance is scored against.

## Impact Baselines

NIST 800-53 defines three security baselines:

| Baseline | Use Case |
|----------|----------|
| **Low** | Minimal security requirements, low-impact systems |
| **Moderate** | Standard security, moderate-impact systems |
| **High** | Enhanced security, high-impact systems (FedRAMP High) |

## Using NIST 800-53 in ControlWeave

### Activate the Framework
1. Go to **Frameworks** → **Add Framework**
2. Select **NIST 800-53 Rev 5**
3. Choose your impact baseline (Low/Moderate/High)
4. Click **Activate**

### Tailor Controls
Mark controls as Not Applicable if they don't apply to your environment:
1. Open the control
2. Click **Mark as N/A**
3. Provide a tailoring justification

### Crosswalk Mappings
NIST 800-53 crosswalks available in ControlWeave:
- NIST 800-53 ↔ ISO 27001:2022
- NIST 800-53 ↔ SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria
- NIST 800-53 ↔ CMMC 2.0
- NIST 800-53 ↔ FedRAMP
